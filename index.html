Our Story

Founded in 1985, Vestvale has been helping families find their perfect vintage property while preserving the charm and character of bygone eras. What started as a small real estate agency has grown into a full-service vintage home specialist.

We understand that a home is more than just walls and a roof - it's a living space that tells a story. That's why we've expanded our services to include carefully curated vintage home decor that complements the architectural beauty of older properties.
